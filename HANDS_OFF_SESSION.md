# The hands-off session

Five people, one at a time, no coaching. You sit on your hands and write down every moment you
want to speak. Each urge to narrate is a missing label or a missing piece of feedback, and that
list is the next round of work. The first coached session (2026-10-10) gave about twenty of them
for free; this is how to get the next twenty without the coaching.

## Before you start

- One person, one device, their own account, a quiet room. No screen share of the password step:
  if you must record, record from a second camera or stop recording for that step.
- Say this, and nothing else: *"I'm going to give you five things to do. I won't help. If you get
  stuck, say what you're thinking and keep going. Nothing you do is wrong; anything that confuses
  you is our problem, not yours."*
- Open a page (this file, or a notebook) with two columns: **what I wanted to say** and
  **what they did instead**.

## The five tasks

Read each one out once, in these words, and then say nothing.

1. *"Make yourself an account and get to a point where you'd be happy to close the laptop."*
2. *"Find the message I sent you."* (Send one direct message before the session, from your own
   account, with a plain sentence in it.)
3. *"Join the group I invited you to."* (Send a direct invite before the session.)
4. *"Make the app dark."* (Or light, if they chose dark.)
5. *"Make sure you could get back into this account on a new phone."*

Stop a task when they say they're done, when they give up, or after five minutes.

## While you watch

Write down, for each task:

- every sentence you wanted to say, verbatim ("it's the second one", "that's your key", "no, go
  back") — this is the finding;
- the first thing they clicked, and whether it was what they meant;
- any time they said "I think it's broken" or "did I do that?";
- what they called things in their own words ("password", "backup", "the code", "the bell").

Don't write down what you think the fix is yet.

## After each person

- Did they finish each task? Yes / gave up / thought they had (check: a key file on disk, the
  message open, the group joined, the theme changed, a saved key).
- Copy the "wanted to say" column into one list. After five people, sort it by how many people
  made you want to say it.

## What to do with the list

Every line on it is a label, a feedback line, or a sentence that belongs on the screen. Fix the
ones three or more people caused first. Then run five more people. The session is cheap on
purpose: one hour each, no tooling, so it survives a week when there is no time.
