# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Relay Outpost to be a welcoming, friendly, and harassment-free project for everyone,
regardless of background or experience level. Maintainers and contributors are expected to treat
each other with respect.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that helps the community:

- Being kind, patient, and welcoming to newcomers.
- Respecting differing viewpoints and giving constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its users.

Behavior that is not acceptable includes harassment of any kind, personal attacks, and other
conduct that would reasonably make others feel unwelcome or unsafe.

## Our standard reference

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its full code of conduct. The summary above is a quick reference; the linked document governs
in full.

## Enforcement

Concerns may be reported privately to the maintainers at **info@megistus.xyz**. All reports
will be reviewed and handled with discretion. Maintainers may take any action they deem
appropriate, up to and including removing contributions or barring participation.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces (the repository, issues, pull requests,
and discussions) and when an individual is representing the project in public spaces.
